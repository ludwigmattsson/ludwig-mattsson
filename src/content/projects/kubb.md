KUBB is the result of the collaboration between Studio Reko and fine carpenter Dennis Holm. The initial idea was to create something that feels timeless, natural and eye-catching. We wanted to make an object that can be used in a variety of ways more than we wanted to make just a piece of furniture.KUBB is ready to be used as a stool, but can also work as a side table beside the sofa.
