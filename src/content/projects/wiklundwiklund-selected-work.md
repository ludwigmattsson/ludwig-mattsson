‘WW – Selected Works’ is a growing series of printed objects summarizing artist-duo wiklundwiklunds work over the years. Each object summarizes one project or several within the same discipline. The series aims to explore different formats, materiality and techniques while maintaining a uniform visual expression.

Studio Reko were commissioned to create the visual concept and the first four publications in the series.
