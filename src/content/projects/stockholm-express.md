Stockholm Express is a short film about two friends who push their luck. Based on the real events of clock robberies in Stockholm in 2019.

Written and Directed by Ludwig Mattsson & Jesper Schröder
Cast Simon Eriksson, Sebastian Vasquez Nilsson, Erland Jacobsen & Anton Winberg
Cinematography Ludwig Mattsson
Sound recording Jesper Schröder
Edited by Ludwig Mattsson & Jesper Schröder

Release 2021
