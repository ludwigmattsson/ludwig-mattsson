GANT is a international clothing brand owned by Switzerland’s largest retail group Maus Fréres that was established in the US in 1949 as GANT Shirt Makers. GANT has nearly 600 stores world-wide in over 70 countries.

The assignment was to create an icon set and guidelines for GANT's digital platforms. The icon set is based on the design principles of GANT's already existing visual identity.
