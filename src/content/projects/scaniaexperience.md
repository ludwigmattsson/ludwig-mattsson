My role involved both Direction of video and Design lead of the application.

Description: Experience Scania’s electric journey in an intuitive, spatial setting designed for Apple Vision Pro. Walk around a life-sized electric truck. Discover how the electric motor powers the vehicle. Track your fleet, and see where drivers are located. You’ll also find a spatial film captured in Scania’s own climate tunnel. This is an introduction to our technology, our vehicles – and our commitment to driving the shift towards sustainable transport.
