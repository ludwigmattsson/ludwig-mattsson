PJADAD and Ludwig Mattsson has developed an architectural structure working as a platform for working, eating and drinking.
The structure is based on a basic shape, a cube that measures 40x40x40 cm (CC). The questions surrounding the project has revolved around the choice of either standing firm as a concept or letting aesthetic formulas rule the process, leading to a continuous dialogue during the construction. Because of the unusual and asymmetric shape of the space, the result is a product of this dialogue and compromise.
